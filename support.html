LiDAR mode says "Needs a Pro model"

LiDAR is built into iPhone Pro and iPad Pro models. On other devices use Map mode, which works on every iPhone and iPad.

It says "Out of range" or "Too close"

LiDAR reads from about 30 cm (1 ft) to about 5 m (16 ft). Past that, switch to Map mode. Glass, mirrors, water and direct sunlight can also stop a reading.

The number will not lock

Hold the phone still and aim at something larger. A small or moving target near the edge of LiDAR's reach does not give a steady reading; move closer.

Map mode asks for Precise Location

A map distance runs from where you stand, so an approximate location would make it a guess. Turn on Precise Location for Range Finder in your device's Settings.

Area and perimeter

In Map mode switch from Distance to Area and tap the corners in order. Three or more give the area and the distance round the edge. Undo corner removes the last one.

Apple Watch

Tap a target in Map mode on your iPhone, or save a map reading, and it appears on your Watch. Open the Watch app once after installing it so the phone can reach it.

The unlock and restoring it

Five saved readings are free. The one-time unlock removes the limit and adds share cards. On a new device, open the Unlock screen and tap Restore purchase.

How accurate is it?

LiDAR readings show their own figure: about 2 cm up close, about 1% further out. A map reading is as good as your GPS fix, which the app shows, and the map imagery. Range Finder is not a laser rangefinder and is not for ballistics or surveying.

Privacy Policy

Range Finder, the LiDAR and map distance app.

Last updated 11 October 2026

Your readings stay on your device. Range Finder has no account and no server of its own. What you measure and save is never sent to us.

What the app stores, and where

  • The readings you save (name, note, distance or area, date, and for a map reading the two ends or the corners). These are kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad, and in your device's own backups if you have those on.
  • Your settings, such as the unit you chose.
  • If you use the Apple Watch app, your map targets are passed from your iPhone to your own paired Watch.

Camera and LiDAR

The camera and the LiDAR sensor are used only while LiDAR mode is open, to show the view and read the distance. No picture, video or depth data is saved or sent anywhere.

Location

Map mode uses your location to measure from where you stand, and the Watch app uses the Watch's location to count down the distance. Your location is used on the device. We never receive it.

What leaves your device

The satellite map is Apple Maps. To draw it, your device asks Apple for map imagery of the area on screen, under Apple's own privacy policy. A share card goes only where you choose to send it. Nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is bought through Apple. We do not see your payment details or your Apple Account.

Usage information, advertising and tracking

There is none. The app has no analytics, no advertising identifier, no ads and no tracking across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Delete a reading in the app and it is gone. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on that device.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date above.

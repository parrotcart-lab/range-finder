Range Finder

Measure a distance two ways, on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Close up, point the phone and read the distance from its LiDAR sensor. For anything farther, tap the target on a satellite map. Measuring is free, there are no ads and there is no account.

What it does

  • Reads the distance to what is under the crosshair with LiDAR, up to about 5 m (16 ft), on iPhone Pro and iPad Pro models
  • Measures any distance on a satellite map from where you stand to a spot you tap
  • Splits a tilted reading into level distance and rise
  • Measures the area and perimeter of a lot or a field from corners you tap
  • Saves readings with a name and a note; five are free, a one-time unlock removes the limit and adds share cards
  • Shows the distance to your target on Apple Watch as you walk

What it is not

Range Finder is not a laser rangefinder and is not for ballistics, surveying or any use where a wrong number could cause harm. LiDAR readings are good to about 2 cm up close; map readings are only as good as your GPS fix and the map imagery.
